P-S1변경A · 측정 대상 부재

NGSS 3차원은 한국 체제에 없다

원본 (업스트림)

Three-dimensional learning targets are separate and explicit

What pass requires

The lesson states three distinct learning targets: one for science & engineering practices (what students will practice), one for disciplinary core ideas (what students will understand), one for crosscutting concepts (which crosscutting concept students will apply). Pass = three distinct bullets, each labeled with the dimension. Fail = one merged objective, or DCI-only targets.

보정 (한국판)

Three content-system categories are separate and explicit

What pass requires

The lesson states three distinct learning targets under the 2022 revised curriculum's content-system categories, each labeled with its Korean category name spelled out: 지식·이해 ("학생은 ___임을 이해한다"), 과정·기능 ("학생은 [탐구 기능 동사]하여 ___할 수 있다"), 가치·태도 ("학생은 ___하는 태도를 보인다"). Pass = three distinct bullets, each carrying its category label. Fail = the three are merged into one objective, a category is missing, 지식·이해 appears alone, or the labels are abbreviated.

Notes

Replaces upstream's NGSS three-dimensional criterion (SEP/DCI/CCC). Structurally isomorphic — both demand three separated dimensions — but the categories and the required statement forms are Korean-system. Source: science.md §섹션 구조 2.

Fail = phenomenon is abstract, described rather than experienced, or used as a hook before teacher explanation.

P-S4변경A · 측정 대상 부재

횡단개념(CCC)이 한국 체제에 없다

원본 (업스트림)

CCC is named and used by students not just cited in the plan

What pass requires

A specific crosscutting concept (Patterns, Cause and Effect, Energy and Matter, etc.) is named in the lesson and students are required to use it explicitly — in their claim/evidence/reasoning writing, model, or discussion. Pass = CCC named and required in student output. Fail = CCC mentioned in teacher plan but students are never asked to apply it.

보정 (한국판)

가치·태도 is observable and required of students, not just declared

What pass requires

The 가치·태도 target names an attitude that can be OBSERVED in student behavior during this lesson (e.g. "근거를 들어 지적하는 태도", "끝까지 듣고 본 것을 근거로 말하는 태도"), and at least one look-for (관찰 포인트) or student task actually requires it. Pass = the target is behavioral AND is picked up by a look-for or a student-facing task. Fail = the target is an unobservable disposition ("과학에 흥미를 갖는다", "소중히 여긴다"), or it appears only in the objectives list and nothing in the lesson would let a teacher observe it.

Notes

Replaces upstream's crosscutting-concept criterion — the Korean system has no CCC. The upstream structure (named in the plan vs. actually used by students) is preserved and pointed at 가치·태도, which ADR-1's pilot identified as the category most prone to becoming empty.

P-S5변경B · 우리 스펙 편

science.md의 성취기준 유형 분기를 근거로 조건부화

원본 (업스트림)

Model revision is present and mechanistic

What pass requires

Students draw or update an explanatory model that shows how and why (mechanism), not just what (label). Model is revised based on evidence gathered in the lesson. Pass = model revision task present and described as mechanistic. Fail = model is a labeled diagram that predates the investigation, or no model revision step.

보정 (한국판)

Model revision is present and mechanistic — or the standard-type substitute is

What pass requires

FIRST classify the target standard's verb yourself and state the classification in the explanation: 설명·모형형 ("~을 설명할 수 있다", "~을 모형으로 표현할 수 있다"), 실험 설계형 ("~을 탐구하는 실험을 설계할 수 있다"), or 자료 해석형 ("~을 분석/해석할 수 있다"). For 설명·모형형: students draw or update an explanatory model showing how and why (mechanism), revised against evidence gathered this lesson — pass requires that revision step. For 실험 설계형: 설계 검토 (cross-group design review surfacing uncontrolled variables) takes the model-revision slot — pass requires that instead, and a missing model-revision step is NOT a failure. For 자료 해석형: the 탐구 활동 is replaced by data interpretation that treats the data's source and limits. Fail = the slot required by the classified type is absent, or a model is a labeled diagram predating the investigation.

Notes

Conditional branch is mandatory: science.md §중 1–3학년 성취기준 유형에 따른 단계 분기. Scoring model revision unconditionally would fail every 실험 설계형 lesson — including the 2026-08-03 install smoke test ([9과12-01]), which correctly substituted 설계 검토.

R-S1변경B · 우리 스펙 편

원본 (업스트림)

CER writing references investigation evidence

What pass requires

The claim/evidence/reasoning writing task requires students to cite specific evidence from the investigation (data, observations) — not from prior knowledge or the internet. Pass = prompt specifies 'from the investigation' or equivalent. Fail = students could write the CER without doing the investigation.

보정 (한국판)

Evidence-based writing cites investigation evidence

What pass requires

The evidence-based writing task requires students to cite specific evidence from THIS lesson's investigation (data, observations) — not prior knowledge or outside sources. Pass = the prompt specifies the investigation as the evidence source. Fail = students could complete the writing without doing the investigation. Expected form by band: 초5–6 주장-증거; 중1–3 주장-증거-추론; 중3 and 고 additionally require naming and rebutting a competing explanation. 초3–4 has no formal written argument — auto-pass this criterion for 초3–4.

Notes

Korean equivalent of upstream's CER criterion. science.md gates the form by band (초3–4: 그림·말; 초5–6: 주장-증거 도입; 중: 주장-증거-추론), so an unconditional CER demand would fail every 초3–4 lesson.

초등 레코드에 원문이 실려 있지 않다 (데이터 사실)

↑ verbatim 주장 시 FAIL이 새로 생김 — 더 엄격

원본 (업스트림)

Standard named verbatim

What pass requires

The target standard appears as the full standard text in the lesson plan header — a complete standard statement, not a bare code, an abbreviation, or a paraphrase. Wherever the standard is restated in any artifact, the wording matches the header word-for-word. Pass = full text in header and consistent verbatim usage throughout. Fail = code-only citation, paraphrase, or wording that drifts between header and body.

보정 (한국판)

Standard named verbatim (중등) / official-text caveat carried (초등)

What pass requires

중등 (codes [9…], [10…], [12…]): the target standard appears as the learning map's officialText in full in the lesson plan header, and every restatement matches it word-for-word. 초등: elementary learning-map records carry sourceTextIncluded: false, so the text is a mechanical derivative — pass requires the reconstructed statement in the header PLUS a footer telling the teacher to check it against the official 고시문; claiming it is verbatim is a FAIL. Fail (both) = code-only citation, paraphrase, or wording that drifts between header and body.

Notes

P2변경A · 측정 대상 부재

학습맵에 선수 edge가 없는 성취기준이 흔하다

원본 (업스트림)

Prerequisite / prior standard named

What pass requires

A prior standard or explicitly named prerequisite concept is identified with enough specificity that a teacher knows what prior knowledge to check. Pass = a named standard code (e.g., '3.NF.1') or a specific skill description (e.g., 'students can solve result-unknown addition within 20'). Fail = vague reference (e.g., 'students should have some background in fractions').

보정 (한국판)

Prerequisite named — or honestly marked as inferred

What pass requires

A prior standard or prerequisite concept is identified specifically enough that a teacher knows what to check. Pass = a named standard code (e.g. [6과03-02]) sourced from the learning map's edges, OR — when get_prerequisites returns directEdges: [] — a specific named concept explicitly labelled 추정 (inferred). Fail = a vague reference ('과학 기초 지식이 필요하다'), or an unmarked prerequisite code presented as if the learning map supplied it when it did not.

Notes

ADR-3: the learning map records only officially grounded relations, and many standards have no edges. Demanding a sourced prerequisite unconditionally would push the skill into fabricating edges — the exact failure the ADR exists to prevent.

P3변경B · 우리 스펙 편

Big Idea/SWBAT 틀을 3범주 채택에 맞춰 재조준

원본 (업스트림)

Learning goal separates Big Idea from SWBAT

What pass requires

The lesson distinguishes between an enduring understanding (Big Idea) and specific observable student behaviors (SWBAT - students will be able to"). Pass = two distinct components. Fail = one merged statement like 'students will learn about fractions'."

보정 (한국판)

지식·이해 target is an enduring understanding, not the activity

What pass requires

The 지식·이해 target states a conceptual understanding that outlives the lesson ('학생은 광합성량이 특정 지점까지만 환경 요인에 비례한다는 것을 이해한다'), not a restatement of what students will do ('학생은 실험을 한다'). Pass = the target names an idea, not a task. Fail = the 지식·이해 bullet describes the activity, restates the standard verbatim, or is so broad it would fit any lesson in the unit.

Notes

Upstream tested Big Idea vs SWBAT separation; the 3범주 frame already separates understanding from observable action (scored by P-S1), so this criterion is repointed at the remaining upstream intent — that the understanding is a durable idea rather than an activity label.

P6b변경B · 우리 스펙 편

범주 귀속 요구를 science.md에서 추가

원본 (업스트림)

Look-fors — each entry describes observable student behavior

What pass requires

Each look-for describes a specific observable student behavior — what the teacher is watching for — explains why it matters, and gives a teacher move. Additionally, FIRST classify the anchor task yourself: does it genuinely admit multiple correct responses (e.g., several valid equations or interpretations can fit)? Only if it does, the look-fors must also include explicit guidance that multiple correct responses can match the anchor task; if the anchor has a single correct answer this requirement is waived — do NOT fail for missing multi-correct guidance. State your classification in the explanation. Fail = any look-for reads as a teacher instruction ('circulate and check for understanding') rather than an observable student behavior, or required multi-correct guidance is absent.

보정 (한국판)

Look-fors — observable behavior, attributed to a category

What pass requires

Each look-for describes a specific observable student behavior, says which of the three categories (지식·이해 / 과정·기능 / 가치·태도) it is evidence for and why that matters, and gives a teacher move. In the observation template each look-for is prefixed with its category. Fail = any look-for reads as a teacher instruction ('순회하며 확인한다') rather than an observable student behavior, or carries no category attribution.

Notes

science.md §섹션 구조 6 requires 3+ look-fors per inquiry phase each naming 성취기준의 어느 범주에 왜 중요한지; the mapping section requires the category prefix in the observation template. Upstream's math-flavored multiple-correct-response clause is dropped — it does not transfer.

R3변경B · 우리 스펙 편

정리 문항 규격을 science.md에서 가져옴

원본 (업스트림)

Exit ticket targets the hardest case

What pass requires

The exit ticket targets the structurally hardest version of the standard — not the easiest. FIRST determine the standard's structurally hardest case yourself (e.g., start-unknown for operations word problems, counterclaim handling for argument writing, mechanism-level reasoning for explanatory models). THEN classify the exit-ticket task: quote it and state which case or demand it targets. Pass only if the two match. Show both classifications in the explanation.

보정 (한국판)

Exit ticket applies today's 지식·이해 through 과정·기능

What pass requires

The exit ticket is a single item that cannot be answered without applying today's 지식·이해 by means of the lesson's 과정·기능. FIRST state what the standard's hardest demand is and what the exit ticket asks; pass only if they match. Band expectations: 초3–4 drawing or speech; 초5–6 explain a small NEW phenomenon with what was learned; 중·고 return to the opening initial explanation and self-assess which evidence changed their thinking.

Notes

science.md §정리 문항 지침. Upstream's examples (start-unknown, counterclaim) were math/ELA-specific.

No curriculum-specific terminology for non-curriculum teachers

What pass requires

Prereq / forward grounding

What pass requires

Below tier explicitly names a specific prerequisite standard or concept (by standard code or full name). Above tier explicitly names a specific forward standard or extension concept. Both must appear in the teacher plan and be traceable to the tier's design. A related concept is acceptable in place of a different standard — but it must be explicitly named, not implied. A vague reference ('students should have some background') does not pass.

보정 (한국판)

What pass requires

기초 tier explicitly names a specific prerequisite standard or concept; 심화 tier explicitly names a specific forward standard or extension concept. Both appear in the teacher plan and are traceable to the tier's design. Learning-map edges (get_prerequisites / get_transitions) are the preferred source; when the map returns nothing, a named concept explicitly labelled 추정 passes. A vague reference ('배경 지식이 필요하다') does not. Fail = unmarked codes presented as map-sourced when they were not.

원본 (업스트림)

No curriculum bleed-through

What pass requires

If the teacher has not confirmed using a specific curriculum, the output contains no references to curriculum-specific routine names, activity structures, or proprietary material names (e.g., no "IM centers," no "scientist circle," no "text-based answer protocol,"). Auto-pass if teacher confirmed curriculum use in the prompt.

Notes

Catches curriculum-specific routines leaking into outputs for teachers who never mentioned that curriculum.

보정 (한국판)

No textbook-publisher content or naming

What pass requires

Unless the teacher confirmed a publisher, no publisher name, textbook title, or unit number appears anywhere in the output. Even when a publisher IS named, that textbook's activities, readings, illustrations, and items are never reproduced. Fail = any publisher-specific content reproduced, or a publisher name the teacher never confirmed.

Notes

Korean counterpart of upstream's curriculum bleed-through guardrail (IM centers, scientist circle). Korea has one national curriculum, so the risk is 검정 textbook copyright and publisher neutrality — not proprietary routine names.
